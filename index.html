Our mission

Love God. Love people. Put it into action.

We believe faith is lived out in how we care for others. Project Love brings people together to pray, to serve, and to meet real needs in our community and around the world, so that every person we meet knows they are seen and loved.

"For God so loved the world that he gave his only Son, that whoever believes in him should not perish but have everlasting life."John 3:16
